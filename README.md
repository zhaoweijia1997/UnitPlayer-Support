# UnitPlayer-Support
Privacy Policy for UnitPlayer (iPhone and Android)

Effective date: October 25, 2026

UnitPlayer is a video player made by UnitApp. It does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app keeps stays on your device.

## What the app accesses, and why

- **Videos on your device.** UnitPlayer asks for access to your videos (on Android: "Photos and videos" or storage access; on iPhone: the photo library) only to list them, show thumbnails and play them. Your videos are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
- **Folders you choose.** If you add a folder, the app reads the videos in that folder. It deletes a file only when you ask it to.
- **Your local network and the internet.** These are used only to connect to servers you add or that are found on your local network (SMB, WebDAV, FTP and DLNA media servers), and to play links you open, so you can browse and stream your own videos. Network discovery looks for such servers and TVs on your Wi-Fi; the results stay on your device.
- **Server logins.** Usernames and passwords for your servers are stored encrypted on your device (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and are sent only to the server they belong to.
- **Playing on a TV.** When you cast to a TV on your Wi-Fi, the app sends that TV the video's address. For videos on your phone or on a server that needs a login, the phone shares that one video (and its subtitle file) with that TV over your local network, at a random address, only while casting.
- **Wi-Fi transfer.** When you open Wi-Fi transfer, the phone accepts video files from a computer on the same Wi-Fi at a random address shown on the screen, only while that window is open. Received videos are saved to your phone's Movies/UnitPlayer folder.

## Buying the developer a coffee (Android)

Settings › About has an optional "Buy the Developer a Coffee". It is paid entirely through Google Play's billing system and unlocks nothing; the app works the same either way.

- Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details, name or email. Google tells the app only that a purchase went through, so it can say thank you.
- The app talks to Google Play's billing service only while that card is open (or once at start, if a payment was still pending). Google's billing library may send Google basic diagnostic information about that connection. This is handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we don't receive it.
- The app keeps no record of purchases. Your purchase history is in your Google Play account.

## What stays on your device

Playback positions, settings and your list of servers are stored on your device only. You can clear the playback history in Settings, and everything is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Third parties

UnitPlayer contains no advertising or analytics services and shares no data with third parties. Payments for the optional coffee are processed by Google Play, as described above.

## Children

UnitPlayer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
