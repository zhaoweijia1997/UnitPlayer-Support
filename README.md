# UnitPlayer-Support
Privacy Policy for UnitPlayer (iPhone and Android)

Effective date: October 29, 2026

UnitPlayer is a video and music player made by UnitApp. It does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the app keeps stays on your device.

## What the app accesses, and why

- **Videos on your device.** UnitPlayer asks for access to your videos (on Android: "Photos and videos" or storage access; on iPhone: the photo library) only to list them, show thumbnails and play them. Your videos are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
- **Music on your device (Android).** UnitPlayer asks for access to music and audio only to list your songs, show their album pictures and play them. Song tags, lyrics files (.lrc) and album sheets (.cue) are read on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and lyrics are never looked up on the internet.
- **Folders you choose.** If you add a folder, the app reads the videos or music in that folder, and the subtitles, lyrics, album sheets and pictures beside them. It deletes a file only when you ask it to.
- **Your local network and the internet.** These are used only to connect to servers you add or that are found on your local network (SMB, WebDAV, FTP and DLNA media servers), and to play links you open, so you can browse and stream your own videos and music. Network discovery looks for such servers and TVs on your Wi-Fi; the results stay on your device.
- **Server logins.** Usernames and passwords for your servers are stored encrypted on your device (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and are sent only to the server they belong to.
- **Playing on a TV.** When you cast to a TV on your Wi-Fi, the app sends that TV the video's address. For videos on your phone or on a server that needs a login, the phone shares that one video (and its subtitle file) with that TV over your local network, at a random address, only while casting.
- **Wi-Fi transfer.** When you open Wi-Fi transfer, the phone accepts video files from a computer on the same Wi-Fi at a random address shown on the screen, only while that window is open. Received videos are saved to your phone's Movies/UnitPlayer folder.

## Playing in the background (Android)

Music, and a video's sound if you choose, keep playing when you leave the app. Android shows this as a notification with playback controls; it also appears on the lock screen and works with headset, Bluetooth and car buttons. Nothing about it leaves your device. With a USB DAC (digital-to-analog converter), the app may ask Android to set the DAC to the playing song's format; that setting stays on your device.

## Rating the app (Android)

After you have used the app for a while, Google Play's own rating card may appear once in a while, so you can rate the app without leaving it. It is shown and handled entirely by Google Play under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we don't receive your rating or review through the app. Settings › About also has a link to the app's Google Play page.

## Buying the developer a coffee (Android)

Settings › About has an optional "Buy the Developer a Coffee". It is paid entirely through Google Play's billing system and unlocks nothing; the app works the same either way.

- Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details, name or email. Google tells the app only that a purchase went through, so it can say thank you.
- The app talks to Google Play's billing service only while that card is open (or once at start, if a payment was still pending). Google's billing library may send Google basic diagnostic information about that connection. This is handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we don't receive it.
- The app keeps no record of purchases. Your purchase history is in your Google Play account.

## What stays on your device

Playback positions, your music queue and playlists, how often songs were played, lyrics timing adjustments, settings and your list of servers are stored on your device only. You can clear the playback history in Settings, and everything is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Third parties

UnitPlayer contains no advertising or analytics services and shares no data with third parties. Payments for the optional coffee and the optional rating card are handled by Google Play, as described above. The app includes open-source libraries (listed in Settings › About › Open-source licenses), among them FFmpeg for some audio formats; they run on your device and send nothing anywhere.

## Children

UnitPlayer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
